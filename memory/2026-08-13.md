### Load-bearing implementation gotchas found while building

1. `directional_fd(fp32_subtract=True)` originally DOWNCAST fp64 evaluations to
   fp32, inflating FD error ~20x at eps=1e-3 and destroying the O(h^2)
   convergence signature. Promote low precision only, never demote. Caught by a
   unit test, not by inspection.
2. **`LabelEmbedder` resamples classifier-free-guidance label dropout on EVERY
   forward in train mode, and it is the ONLY stochasticity in this
   architecture** (timm Attention/Mlp are built with drop=0). Uncontrolled,
   `phi(z+hu)` and `phi(z-hu)` get INDEPENDENT conditioning and the FD numerator
   measures dropout noise instead of the derivative -- an error that grows
   without bound as h -> 0. `frozen_label_dropout()` samples the mask once per
   step. (`fb_direct/exact_hvp.py:compute_field_direct` already did the
   equivalent `_predrop_labels` -- the repo knew, the knowledge wasn't written
   down anywhere findable.)
3. Fused SDPA kernels have NO double-backward: any arm differentiating through
   `grad_z E` must disable flash/mem-efficient SDPA exactly as train.py does,
   or it dies with `aten::_scaled_dot_product_efficient_attention_backward is
   not implemented`. Killed job 39062911 mid-run.
4. Storing n full B/2 parameter gradients OOMs (130M params, ~1 GiB each in
   fp64; killed 39063725). Two streaming vectors give the same statistics
   EXACTLY: `E|g-gbar|^2 = E|g|^2 - |gbar|^2` and, for the mean pairwise cosine,
   `(|sum_i g_i/|g_i||^2 - n) / (n(n-1))`. Verified against brute force to 1e-13.
5. macOS `multiprocessing.Pool` deadlocks when a `Lock` is passed through a
   spawned Pool initializer -- use independent sharded processes writing
   per-shard JSONL instead.
