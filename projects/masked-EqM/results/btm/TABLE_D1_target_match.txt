READING (new, 2026-08-17)
=========================
The headline "G 0.7046 vs V 0.7081" hides a systematic ASYMMETRY that the split
columns expose:

                     cos far      cos near_data
  V vector BTM        0.7180         0.6047
  G scalar exact      0.7200         0.5602      <- G is HIGHER far, LOWER near

G matches (in fact slightly exceeds) the vector arm on the FAR field, and is
worse than it only NEAR the data manifold, by 0.045 -- a gap ~13x the far-field
gap of 0.002, and stable across every step-matched window from 11.7k to 75k
(near: 0.6394/0.5441, 0.5804/0.5341, 0.6028/0.5547, 0.6047/0.5602).

This localizes the cost of the conservative parametrization. b_theta = grad_x
phi_theta is not uniformly worse at fitting the corrected BTM target; it is
worse specifically where the target is most structured, which is also where
sample quality is determined. It is the same region the legacy EqM target
degenerates in (c(gamma) -> 0 as gamma -> 1). That two different scalar
formulations both lose accuracy in the same region is a hint that the difficulty
is a property of representing this field as a gradient near the manifold, not an
artifact of either objective.

CAVEAT: this is a within-arm eval diagnostic, not a sample-quality measurement.
It predicts where FID differences should appear if they appear; it does not
establish that they do. Table D (FID at the frozen T=3.0 operating point) is
what tests it, and is blocked on the holylfs06 val split.

D1/D4 plateau at ~0.61 against V/G's 0.70-0.71, confirming the FD variance
ceiling from TABLE_B. K=4 is better than K=1 early (0.6106 at 11k vs 0.5692 at
11.7k), consistent with lower estimator variance, but D1 at 66.9k still only
reaches 0.6061 -- more directions help and do not close the gap.

E_mean ~ +1099 with E_std ~ +1188 for G confirms the potential is NOT calibrated,
as pre-registered ("do not interpret E as calibrated -log p_data"). Only its
gradient is claimed to be meaningful.

The two LEGACY arms emit no target_cosine records at all -- they are not trained
against the corrected BTM target, so there is no b* to compare to. They appear
in TABLE_C (optimizer behaviour, which is arm-agnostic) and cannot appear here.
